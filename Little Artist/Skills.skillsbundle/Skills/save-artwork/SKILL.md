# Save a child's artwork

1. If the request names a child, find that child and pass them to the Capture Artwork tool. Otherwise run the Capture Artwork tool without a child; the app asks or uses the child currently selected.
2. The Capture Artwork tool opens Little Artist ready to photograph or scan the piece. Tell the person the camera is ready.
3. Don't invent titles or captions. Little Artist suggests them on the device after the photo is taken.

If Little Artist says the free trial has ended, explain that saving new artwork needs the one-time lifetime unlock, and that existing artwork can still be viewed and exported.

## Examples

- "Save Mia's drawing in Little Artist" → Capture Artwork with child Mia.
- "Add this painting to Little Artist" → Capture Artwork with no child.
